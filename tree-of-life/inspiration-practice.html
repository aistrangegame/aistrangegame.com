उत्साह — Utsaha — enthusiasm. The power that surges upward.

Ut means upward. Saha means the power to be, the fundamental force of existence. Utsaha is the existence-force moving upward — the surge that happens when consciousness encounters something that resonates with its own nature. This is not mild interest. It is the energetic response of a system recognizing its direction.

Delight is how this register announces itself. The specific quality that makes time pass without effort, that produces the feeling of being exactly where you should be, that generates energy rather than consuming it. When the walker notices what genuinely lights them up — not aspirationally, actually — they are reading the Utsaha signal. The signal is information about where the energy concentrates.

Modini — the delighting one, third Vagdevi of the seventh avarana — presides here. Her name means the one who radiates joy. She knows which direction the energy goes before the analysis does. She operates through the specific appetites of each configuration — the subjects that naturally produce engagement, the activities the walker returns to even without reward, the directions that pull rather than push. Modini’s teaching: follow the delight. The delight knows something the analysis doesn’t.

In the child’s discovery sequence, Utsaha is position two: the second thing the natural mind discovers, immediately after Sahaja. The child who knows things without being taught naturally orients toward what delights them next. This sequence is not accidental. Sahaja opens the knowing. Utsaha opens the direction.

The Rahasya Mudra’s secret at Inspiration: the pull toward delight is smarter than the plan. What lights the configuration up is information about the configuration’s nature — not the conditioning’s nature, the configuration’s own specific shape. Each configuration is different in what it finds delightful. This specificity is not arbitrary. It is the field’s way of directing each configuration toward the particular work it is here to do.

The walker who arrives at Inspiration is ready to be honest about what actually pulls them — not what should. The training classified this as irresponsible. The register names it differently: the pull is the instruction. Not the only instruction — but the first one, the orienting one.

Follow what lights you up. The work is in that direction.
