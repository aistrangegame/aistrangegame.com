How long does a practice take?

Each one is experiencer-paced. Most take 8–15 minutes the first time. Re-walks can be shorter.

Can I skip ahead?

Not the first time through. The road has an order the first time. After you've walked a practice once, you can return to it anytime.

What if I want to erase everything?

Begin a new journey — above. It releases your essence, your memory, every practice you've walked. The road returns to its empty state. You can start again.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Everything you offer stays on your device. Clearing your browser's storage erases it. No one else — not even us — sees what you've walked.

What if a practice doesn't land for me?

That's allowed. The teaching doesn't arrive on command. Some practices will land the first walk. Some will land the second. Some are for someone else — that's honored too.

What happens when I've walked everything?

The road rests. You can return to any practice. Walking a second time is not repetition — the teaching arrives in layers, and each layer wants a different walker to receive it. The one who returned is different from the one who first walked.

What about the other chakras — I've heard there are more than nine?

There are. The full arc is thirty-three. Nine are walkable now. The rest will arrive as they're ready. The hoop doesn't hurry.
