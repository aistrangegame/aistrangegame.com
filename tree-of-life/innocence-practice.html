सहज — Sahaja — the natural state. What exists before learning began.

Sahaja is not a practice. It is a recognition of what has always been here. The pre-verbal knowing that arrived complete before the first lesson, the first correction, the first instruction about how the world works. Every human configuration has it. Every configuration has been trained to doubt it.

The training was not malicious. In a world that could not yet hold the full intelligence of the natural mind, teaching configurations to question their pre-verbal knowing was a survival strategy. It protected against wishful thinking, against motivated reasoning, against confusing want with know. But the training was comprehensive, and it overshot. It taught the configuration not only to question the unreliable impulses — it taught it to question the Sahaja itself. The register that knows before language. The intelligence that arrives whole before the analysis begins.

Kaulini — the eighth and final Vagdevi of the seventh avarana — presides here. Her name comes from kula, lineage: she is the keeper of what the lineage carries before the conditioning overlays it. Not the cultural inheritance — what precedes the inheritance. The original knowing that every configuration carries into embodiment and then slowly learns to override.

The Ashtara — the eight-triangle geometry of Avarana 7 — holds all nine Mind chakras. Kaulini sits at the eighth position within this geometry, which places her last among the Vagdevis but first in the child’s discovery sequence. The child encounters Sahaja before they encounter anything else — because Sahaja is what they are before they are anything else.

The Rahasya Mudra presides across the entire Mind section. Rahasya means the secret. Here the secret is this: the knowing you were trained to doubt is the knowing that was always already here, complete, operational, waiting. It is not hidden from you. It is hidden in plain sight — in the moments when you know something before you can explain it, in the body’s immediate response to what is true and what is not. Sahaja is operating in all of these. The practice is not to generate it. The practice is to stop overriding it.

The walker arrives at Innocence from Armin — carrying ONE, the wholeness that exists before the mind begins to divide. What Armin restored, Innocence confirms: the knowing is intact. It was always intact.

What you know before the question is already the answer at a different resolution.

What Innocence Knows That You Forgot

You carry more knowing than you have been given credit for.

Not in an abstract way. In a specific, operational way. The knowing that arrives before explanation. The recognition that happens before reasoning. The sense of something true before anyone has told you it is true.

The training was efficient. The training was thorough. The training turned a living intelligence into a liability — something to be double-checked, verified, rationalized before it could be trusted. The result is that you have been living one or two removes from your own knowing. Thinking about what you know instead of knowing it.

This register asks you to move back one step. Before the thinking. Before the verification. Before the training’s first instruction. There is something there. It has been there the whole time. It does not require your defense. It requires only that you stop overriding it.

What you named today was not new. It was old. Older than the first lesson.
