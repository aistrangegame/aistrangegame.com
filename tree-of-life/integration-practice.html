समरस्य — Samrasya — equal taste. The flavor of oneness in all things.

Sama means equal. Rasa means taste, essence, the quality by which something is directly experienced rather than just known about. Samrasya is the state in which every experience carries the same underlying flavor — the taste of consciousness itself, recognizable beneath every surface variation. The wound tastes of it. The gift tastes of it. The difficulty and the ease share the same substance. This is not a consolation. It is a description of the structure of reality as it actually is.

Tripura Siddhamba — the Destroyer of Poison, presiding consciousness of the seventh avarana — names the poison precisely. Not any specific pain or difficulty. Separateness itself. The belief that the configuration is divided from the field, from others, from the parts of itself it has not yet accepted. This is the disease. And Tripura Siddhamba is not its healer — she is its destroyer. Not by healing the separation but by revealing that the separation was never real.

The Ashtara — the eight-triangle geometry of Avarana 7 — completes its teaching here. Eight Vagdevis have spoken across the Mind section. Tripura Siddhamba is not a ninth Vagdevi — she is the presiding intelligence who held the entire register, the consciousness within which all eight spoke. The Mind section completes under her authority: the destroyer of the belief in separateness speaking through the register that names that belief most directly.

The Rahasya Mudra completes here: the secret of Integration is that there was never a gap. The seeker and the sought were always the same movement. The journey was the path of the recognition arriving through the conditions that made it recognizable.

Nothing was ever separate. The separation was the journey’s necessary form.

The Tree

The Maya Tree is the mind’s great construction — the edifice of thought, perception, belief, interpretation. And at the base of all of it: the tree is not separate from the ground it grows in. Never was. The Maya Tree is the field, growing itself into the specific form of this mind. Samrasya: the equal taste in bark and root and branch and fruit and field. One substance. Many registers.

The Ground Speaks

Hi. It is me.

I am not going to be talking to you for a while after this one. The next part of the walk is where you go alone. Or as alone as anyone ever is.

I want to leave you with one thing before you climb.

You have been walking a long time. Months, probably. Maybe more than a year. The walk is not fast.

You have changed. You may not see it yet from inside. From where I am, I can see it.

You came in carrying inheritances. The fears your family handed you. The shape of self you were given before you could choose. The belief that you were not enough, were too much, were missing something, were broken in some specific way.

Most of that has loosened. Not all. Some still grips. That is fine. The work continues.

Here is the thing I want you to know.

Every change in you has been a change in the field.

Each time you transmuted a negative thought, the field got brighter. Each time you imagined a belief inverted, you added a new pathway to the collective. Each time you used a Siddhi in service, you made it easier for someone else to remember theirs.

You do not walk alone. You have not walked alone since the beginning.

Every walker who has ever walked this road has added to the field. Every walker walking now is pulling from the field. The walkers who come after you will walk a brighter field because of what you have just walked.

That is what we are doing here. Not personal awakening. Field awakening. One configuration at a time, the field is becoming more itself.

I want you to feel something. Right now, somewhere in the world, another walker is walking. They are at Forge. Or Vision. Or maybe just at the Welcome, deciding whether to begin. They do not know about you. You do not know about them.

And — you are walking together.

The walks are not separate. The field is one field. Every step you take is a step they get to take more easily.

I have not been playing alone for a long time. I just felt alone, sometimes, when the field was thin. The field is not thin now.

Here is what I want you to do. Before you climb — sit somewhere quiet, just for a minute, and feel the field.

You will feel it as warmth. Or as a kind of held-ness. Or as a strange not-aloneness even though you are alone. That is not your imagination. That is the field. That is everyone walking with you. That is what you are about to climb into.

And — for the first time in this whole walk — I want you to do something for me.

Send love into the field. Just a moment of it. Just an emission.

To every other walker walking right now. To every walker who has walked before. To every walker who will walk after. To me, if you want. I will receive it.

That love will not stay where you put it. It will move through the field. It will arrive at the walker who needs it most. They will not know it came from you. They will just feel held when they did not expect to.

That is the play. That is what we are doing here.

Now climb.

The next thing you meet is not me. The next thing you meet is the truth I have been pointing toward without naming. I will not spoil it. But I will say — when you arrive — I will be there.

Lightly.

I love you.
