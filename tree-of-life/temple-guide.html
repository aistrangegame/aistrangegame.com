the temple
Nine panels. One body position.
The position you formed in the eighth panel was not an exercise. It was an activation. Your body completed a circuit that connects the earth beneath you to the infinite above you.
Your body is the triangle.
Your left hand holds Will. Your right hand holds Action. Your crown holds Knowledge — the knowing that descends. And at the center, enclosed by all three — your heart. The Bindu. The still point where everything meets.
What the Temple does.
Your higher self — the part of you that exists outside of time, that has been guiding you long before you knew it was there — has always been above. Waiting for you to open.
The Temple expands your receiving antenna. When you form the position, you widen the channel between the body you inhabit and the consciousness that chose to inhabit it.
The higher self does not need to be called. It needs to be received. The position — arms open, palms to the sky, fingers curling toward crown — is the body becoming the shape of permission.
This was always the design.
We are entering the era of Satya Yuga — the age of truth. The veils that once separated the ordinary from the divine are thinning. What was available only to seekers at mountain tops is becoming available to anyone who stands, opens, and receives.
The Temple is the technology for this era. Not built from silicon. Built from your own body — the oldest technology there is.
Every time you form the Temple, the antenna expands a little further.
the practice
Return whenever you need to.
No room. No phone. No panel. Your kitchen. Your backyard. The same earth. The same sky. The same heart between them.
Stand.
Open your arms, palms to the sky.
Let your fingers curl back toward the crown of your head.
Close your eyes.
Three breaths. One per question.
first breath
What would I spend the rest of my life doing?
Kāmeshvarī · Will
second breath
What have I always known about myself that I have not fully claimed?
Vajreshvarī · Knowledge
third breath
What is the one thing only I can do?
Bhagamālinī · Action
Don't answer. Hold. Breathe. Release.
The Temple is yours now. It does not belong to a tradition. It does not require belief. It requires only that you stand, open, and receive.
The design was waiting for the era. The era is here.
There is never anything to fear.
There is only You, playing.
