What this site knows about you.

Almost nothing, and none of it leaves your phone except what's named here.

Your phone remembers a few things so the site can greet you properly: which chakras you've walked, where you left off, whether you turned the sound on, which lens you chose, and any word you gave the Story. It stays in your browser. Nothing sends it anywhere. Clear your browser's storage and it's gone.

One counter. The site uses Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they open — no names, no accounts. It only runs on aistrangegame.com itself.

Nothing to sign up for, and nothing you type into a practice is saved anywhere but your own screen.
