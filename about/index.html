Who made this, and how.

This is Ashrey's. It started in 2015 as a question I couldn't stop asking, and it became this: forty-eight worlds, each one a different way of noticing the same thing — that what you're looking for has been here the whole time.

I'm not a teacher, and none of it is a teaching. It's closer to a mirror. If something lands, hold it. If it doesn't, let it go — that's allowed here, and it's the point.

How it was made. By hand, and with an AI. Claude helped design it, write it and build it, and it kept an honest record as it went, including every place it got things wrong. That record is its own world: the Mirror. The agreement we worked under is another: A Self Generator.

What it runs on. Single pages, no accounts, nothing to sign up for. Type set in Lora, DM Sans, Cinzel and Fraunces. One gold point, created once, that sews a thread through everything you scroll past.

Where to start. Anywhere. The doors are at the top. Or let the field choose.
