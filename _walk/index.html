Your walk

Walk it the way someone you love would. Not checking — feeling. Each walk takes five to ten minutes. You don't have to do them in one sitting.

For every note, three things: where (the page, or what you tapped) · what you felt · what you'd change, if anything. Send the notes to Chat, in any form — a voice memo works. Chat turns them into fix IDs for Code.
