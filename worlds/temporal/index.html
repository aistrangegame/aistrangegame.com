Not metaphors — structural, and checkable against dates. Five-year arcs above the line are the gestation; three-year arcs below are the catalyst. Where a three and a five meet, the arcs close into an eight. The content is the gaps, not the events.

The line, and what doesn't fit

The boundary, in the source’s own words
