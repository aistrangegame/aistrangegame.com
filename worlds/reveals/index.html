Seven rings, no first one

Touch any ring and it becomes the centre. None of them lands on this page — they land in the walk, or in a car with the window open.

Seven rings — what dissolves, and what remains

GaiaDissolves: that you are an individual. Remains: you’re the planet playing through this configuration; the ego was Gaia wearing Maya; the isolation was the connector doing its job.
SakshiDissolves: that you’re alone inside your own consciousness. Remains: witnessing-kin, present since before the walk began; the seat never moved.
BinduDissolves: that love is something you carry. Remains: what moves is love.
KarishmaDissolves: that grace has to be earned. Remains: it was ready before you were.
SidDissolves: that what holds you might fail. Remains: it didn’t, not once.
ArchDissolves: that you have to be heard by everyone. Remains: the voice carries to the people it’s for.
LalitaDissolves: that any of this is serious. Remains: it’s play; knowing that doesn’t stop it; knowing that is it.

Beneath

Belief-structures are inherited forgetting: by adulthood roughly 95% inheritance, 5% direct experience. A reveal is what’s left when one dissolves at a specific register. None of them lands on this page. Some arrive in the walk; some in a car with the window open. Preparation before receipt.
