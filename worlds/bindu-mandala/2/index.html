Sarvasaparipuraka Chakra

All-Fulfilling.

16-petal lotus (Shodasha Dala Padma). Holds the 16 Karshini Shaktis — the attracting powers: the five sense-organs, five elements, five tanmatras drawing the world inward as experience, plus mind. The first ring of the journey inward — where every sense is honored as a goddess.

the Shaktis of this enclosure

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Kāmākarṣiṇī कामाकर्षिणीKāma

    the nameKāma (desire / wish / longing) + ākarṣiṇī (she who attracts) = She who attracts desire — the force that pulls wanting itself into existence. Before any specific want, there is the capacity to want; She is that capacity.

    what she doesDesire, will, love-impulse — the entire spectrum of yearning, motivation, attraction toward the good. The first wave. Without her there is no movement, no life-impulse, no devotion. Kāma turned toward the Divine is devotion itself — hence Kāmeśvarī/Kāmeśvara at the center of the yantra is the primordial pair.

    in the bodyPelvic basin, gut, chest leaning forward

  • 2
    Buddhyākarṣiṇī बुद्ध्याकर्षिणीBuddhi

    the nameBuddhi (intellect / discriminating awareness) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts intellect. The clarity by which one thing is distinguished from another — the faculty of discernment itself drawn into being.

    what she doesDiscernment, intelligence, the cutting edge of viveka — that which distinguishes the real from the apparent. Where Kāma is undirected longing, Buddhi shapes it into a decision. She is the faculty that makes experience intelligible.

    in the bodyForehead, eyes

  • 3
    Ahaṅkārākarṣiṇī अहंकाराकर्षिणीAhaṅkāra

    the nameAhaṅkāra (I-maker / ego-function) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts the I-making. Not vanity — the basic operation of consciousness identifying with itself as a separate self. The function before content.

    what she doesThe I-maker, sense of personal agency, ownership. In Sāṃkhya, ahaṃkāra is the principle from which the senses and elements spring. Both binding agent AND necessary support of embodied life. To be witnessed, not destroyed.

    in the bodySolar plexus, shoulders

  • 4
    Śabdākarṣiṇī शब्दाकर्षिणीŚabda

    the nameŚabda (sound / vibration / word) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts sound. The tanmatra of audition — the subtle quality that becomes hearing in the ear. All language, all music, all silence enters through Her.

    what she doesSound, word, vibration, mantra — the entire sonic world. She is what makes sound capture attention; what makes a single name penetrate the heart. Also 'word/scripture' — the Vedic dimension.

    in the bodyEars, back of skull

  • 5
    Sparśākarṣiṇī स्पर्शाकर्षिणीSparśa

    the nameSparśa (touch / contact / sensation) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts touch. The tanmatra of contact — heat, cold, pressure, texture. The first proof that there is an outside meeting an inside.

    what she doesTouch, texture, temperature, pressure — the whole of haptic intelligence. She is the magnetism by which skin learns the world. Also rules inward touch — the felt sense, interoception, the embrace.

    in the bodyEverywhere skin meets world — palms, lips, soles

  • 6
    Rūpākarṣiṇī रूपाकर्षिणीRūpa

    the nameRūpa (form / shape / visible appearance) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts form. The tanmatra of vision — colour and shape becoming sight. The world appearing as image, light arriving as recognition.

    what she doesForm, visible appearance, beauty, color, geometry — anything seen. She is why the eye lingers on the moon, on a face, on the Śrī Yantra itself.

    in the bodyEyes, front of face

  • 7
    Rasākarṣiṇī रसाकर्षिणीRasa

    the nameRasa (taste / essence / juice / aesthetic flavor) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts taste — and beyond: aesthetic experience itself. Rasa carries double meaning: the taste of food and the taste of beauty. Both Her domain.

    what she doesTaste, juice, essence, aesthetic savor — literal flavour AND the rasa of poetry, meetings, moments. Sanskrit 'rasa' covers taste, bodily liquidity, AND aesthetic relish (the rasa theory of art). The deliciousness in everything.

    in the bodyTongue, throat

  • 8
    Gandhākarṣiṇī गन्धाकर्षिणीGandha

    the nameGandha (smell / scent / fragrance) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts smell. The tanmatra of olfaction — the subtlest of the elemental tanmatras, the one most directly tied to memory and the earth element.

    what she doesScent, fragrance, the olfactory tanmātra — most memory-laden of the senses. She can pull a whole past life into the present through a single inhalation. Smell is uniquely tied to limbic memory.

    in the bodyBridge of nose

  • 9
    Cittākarṣiṇī चित्ताकर्षिणीCitta

    the nameCitta (mind-substance / awareness-field / consciousness-stuff) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts mind. The field in which all the others appear. The basic capacity of awareness to receive experience.

    what she doesThe mind-stuff, the fourth antaḥkaraṇa — the storage layer of impressions/saṃskāras, the substratum of attention. Where buddhi decides and ahaṃkāra claims, citta is the deep pool where saṃskāras settle and where the witness can rest. She magnetises what you stay with.

    in the bodyBehind the heart

  • 10
    Dhairyākarṣiṇī धैर्याकर्षिणीDhairya

    the nameDhairya (courage / steadiness / fortitude) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts courage. Cosmologically essential — without the capacity to stand under experience, no other Shakti's work completes. Load-bearing at the level of reality itself.

    what she doesCourage, steadiness, the unshakeable — the capacity to remain. Cosmologically load-bearing: without her the circuit of experience breaks down. Memory cannot form, names cannot stick, seeds cannot plant. Not merely a virtue — a foundational force of attraction.

    in the bodyBack-body, spine

  • 11
    Smṛtyākarṣiṇī स्मृत्याकर्षिणीSmṛti

    the nameSmṛti (memory / mindfulness / recollection) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts memory. Both ordinary memory (what happened) and yogic memory (what is). Without Her, no continuity — each moment would be unrelated to the last.

    what she doesMemory, recollection, the traditional knowledge that is 'remembered.' Sādhana itself persists only because of her — practice depends on remembering to practise. The body sometimes remembers before the mind does.

    in the bodyEyes turning inward

  • 12
    Nāmākarṣiṇī नामाकर्षिणीNāma

    the nameNāma (name / designation / linguistic identity) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts name. The form by which the formless lets itself be addressed. The dharmic frequency that makes a self callable.

    what she doesName, the sound-form by which a thing is recognised — how the unmanifest becomes particular. Namarupa (name-and-form) is how the formless takes a specific shape. The moment of naming when undifferentiated experience becomes specific.

    in the bodyThroat, whole body settling

  • 13
    Bījākarṣiṇī बीजाकर्षिणीBīja

    the nameBīja (seed / origin / mantric syllable) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts seed. The compressed potential before anything has unfolded. The bridge between named self and Self — what is small enough to be a point and total enough to become a universe.

    what she doesSeed, germ, source-syllable, potential — the source-point of anything. In tantric context every mantra has a bīja, every form has a bīja, every body has a bīja. She finds what wants to germinate.

    in the bodyDeep belly

  • 14
    Ātmākarṣiṇī आत्माकर्षिणीĀtman

    the nameĀtmā (Self / soul / the witness) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts the Self. The pull toward the awareness behind every awareness. What watches without being watched.

    what she doesThe witness-self, the simple sense of being-here — not ego's 'I am the doer' (that is ahaṃkāra) but the deeper 'I am.' The magnetism by which awareness returns to itself.

    in the bodyBody's centre

  • 15
    Amṛtākarṣiṇī अमृताकर्षिणीAmṛta

    the nameAmṛta (deathless / immortal / nectar) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts the deathless. The pull toward what does not pass. Not denial of death but recognition of what remains when bodies do not.

    what she doesNectar, immortality, the soma-drip — what nourishes without depleting, what refreshes, what gives the felt sense of 'this could continue forever.' In yogic physiology connected with the candramaṇḍala at the crown that drips amṛta downward. Immortality and creativity are the same fluid.

    in the bodySoft palate, crown

  • 16
    Śarīrākarṣiṇī शरीराकर्षिणीŚarīra

    the nameŚarīra (body / vessel / form) + ākarṣiṇī = She who attracts the body. The closing of the 16-petal lotus. Her esoteric tattva is manas (mind) — the tantric declaration that the deepest physicality and the most subtle psyche are not two.

    what she doesThe gross body, physical form, embodiment itself — that which decays (śīr = to wither). The very takingness of having a body: its weight, its presence, its location. The final Karshini completes the wheel by returning to the physical.

    in the bodyWhole body, ground

From Sri Vidya, the tradition of the Sri Yantra, as it teaches them.
