Bindu Mandala ·the fourth enclosure

Sarvasaubhagyadayaka Chakra

All-Good-Fortune-Giving.

14 triangles (Chaturdasha Trikona). Holds 14 Sampradaya Shaktis carrying lineage-transmission: Sarva-Sankshobhini, Sarva-Vidravini, Sarva-Akarshini, Sarva-Ahladini, Sarva-Sammohini, Sarva-Sthambhini, Sarva-Jrimbhini, Sarva-Vashankari, Sarva-Ranjini, Sarva-Onmadini, Sarvarthasadhika, Sarva-Sampatpurani, Sarva-Mantramayi, Sarva-Dvandvakshayankari. Names that echo the 1st avarana Mudras — but here as fully-embodied Devis.

the Shaktis of this enclosure

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Sarva-Saṅkṣobhiṇī (Devī) सर्वसंक्षोभिणीCosmic Stirring

    the nameSarva (all) + Saṅkṣobhiṇī (the agitating one) — 'she who agitates all.' As Devi: the cosmic force the 1st-avarana Mudra invokes.

    what she doesThe Devi-form of the 1st-avarana Mudra-Shakti. Where the Mudra was the gesture, She is the awakened field. Cross-linked to Her 1st-avarana counterpart.

    in the bodyWhole field beginning to move

  • 2
    Sarva-Vidrāviṇī (Devī) सर्वविद्राविणीCosmic Flow

    the nameSarva + Vidrāviṇī (the liquefier) — 'she who liquefies all.' Devi-form of the 2nd-opening Mudra.

    what she doesThe liquefying force as goddess. What was rigid becomes living current. Devi-form of the 1st-avarana Mudra. Cross-linked.

    in the bodyJoints, lymph, all fluids

  • 3
    Sarva-Ākarṣiṇī (Devī) सर्वाकर्षिणीUniversal Magnetism

    the nameSarva + Ākarṣiṇī (the all-attractor) — 'she who attracts all.' The cosmic source of the 16 Karshinis.

    what she doesThe originating attractive force as goddess. The 16 Karshinis are Her specific expressions. Cross-linked to the 1st-avarana Mudra and to the 2nd-avarana Karshinis as their mother-form.

    in the bodyHeart / center of gravity

  • 4
    Sarva-Āhlādinī सर्वाह्लादिनीCosmic Joy

    the nameSarva + Āhlāda (joy / delight) + inī — 'she who delights all.' No 1st-avarana counterpart — new to the 4th ring.

    what she doesThe unconditional joy that pulses through being. Not happiness-from-cause — the joy that is the substrate.

    in the bodyFace / chest

  • 5
    Sarva-Sammohinī सर्वसम्मोहिनीDivine Bewilderment

    the nameSarva + Sammohanī (bewilderer / enchanter of senses) — 'she who bewilders all' — the divine sweet confusion that breaks habitual thought.

    what she doesThe blessed confusion that breaks fixed thought. Holy disorientation that precedes deeper seeing.

    in the bodyForehead / behind the eyes

  • 6
    Sarva-Sthambhinī सर्वस्तम्भिनीCosmic Stillness

    the nameSarva + Sthambhinī (paralyzer / stabilizer) — 'she who stabilizes all.' The pause that is also strength.

    what she doesThe stillness within motion. The pause that lets Sthiti settle into itself.

    in the bodyDiaphragm / breath turn

  • 7
    Sarva-Jṛmbhinī सर्वजृम्भिणीCosmic Expansion

    the nameSarva + Jṛmbha (yawn / stretch / expansion) + inī — 'she who expands all,' the yawning-open. Counterpart to Sthambhinī.

    what she doesThe yawn of being. The deep stretch that opens what was closed. Reality breathing wider.

    in the bodyWhole body extending

  • 8
    Sarva-Vaśaṅkarī (Devī) सर्ववशंकरीCosmic Yielding

    the nameSarva + Vaśaṅkarī (the subjugator-yielder) — 'she who brings all under influence.' Devi-form of the 4th-opening Mudra.

    what she doesThe Devi who brings all-under-influence through being so aligned that everything turns naturally. Devi-form of the 1st-avarana Mudra. Cross-linked.

    in the bodyHeart-field / radiance from chest

  • 9
    Sarva-Rañjinī सर्वरञ्जिनीCosmic Coloring

    the nameSarva + Rañj (to color / delight) + inī — 'she who colors all,' the chromatic-aesthetic principle.

    what she doesThe principle that adds color, flavor, depth to experience. Without Her, all would be gray.

    in the bodyEyes / ears / aesthetic field

  • 10
    Sarva-Onmādinī (Devī) सर्वोन्मादिनीCosmic Intoxication

    the nameSarva + Onmādinī (the intoxicator) — 'she who intoxicates all.' Devi-form of the 5th-opening Mudra.

    what she doesThe divine intoxication of being seen by Her. The Devi-form that lifts perception into rasa. Devi-form of the 1st-avarana Mudra. Cross-linked.

    in the bodyThird eye / aesthetic field

  • 11
    Sarvārthasādhikā सर्वार्थसाधिकाCosmic Fulfillment

    the nameSarva + Artha (purpose / aim) + Sādhikā (accomplisher) — 'she who accomplishes all purposes.'

    what she doesThe Devi who brings every aim to its accomplishment. Things complete themselves through Her presence.

    in the bodySolar plexus / center of action

  • 12
    Sarva-Sampatpūraṇī सर्वसम्पत्पूरणीCosmic Abundance

    the nameSarva + Sampat (wealth / abundance) + Pūraṇī (filler) — 'she who fills all with abundance.'

    what she doesThe principle of plenty. Not material wealth alone — the abundance that is reality's nature.

    in the bodyBelly / abundance-field

  • 13
    Sarva-Mantramayī सर्वमन्त्रमयीCosmic Mantra

    the nameSarva + Mantra + Mayī (made-of) — 'she made of all mantras,' the embodied principle of sacred speech.

    what she doesReality recognized as mantric. Every sound, every word, every breath as sacred utterance.

    in the bodyThroat / breath-rhythm

  • 14
    Sarva-Dvandvakṣayaṅkarī सर्वद्वन्द्वक्षयंकरीCosmic Non-Duality

    the nameSarva + Dvandva (duality / pair-of-opposites) + Kṣaya (destruction) + Kāriṇī (maker) — 'she who destroys all pairs of opposites.'

    what she doesThe closing Devi of the 4th avarana. Destroyer of dualities — heat/cold, pleasure/pain, self/other. Prepares the threshold to the inward turn.

    in the bodyHeart / non-dual point

From Sri Vidya, the tradition of the Sri Yantra, as it teaches them.
