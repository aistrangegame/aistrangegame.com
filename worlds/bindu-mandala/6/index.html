Bindu Mandala ·the sixth enclosure

Sarvarakshakara Chakra

All-Protecting.

Inner 10 triangles (Antardasara). Holds 10 Nigarbha (inside-the-womb) Shaktis: Sarvajna, Sarvashakti, Sarvaishwarya-pradayini, Sarvajnana-mayi, Sarva-Vyadhi-vinashini, Sarvadhara-svarupa, Sarva-Papahara, Sarvananda-mayi, Sarva-Rakshasvarupini, Sarva-Epsita-Phalaprada. The protection that comes from being held inside the source — not defense from outside, but recognition that there is no outside.

the Shaktis of this enclosure

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Sarvajñā सर्वज्ञाAll-Knowing

    the nameSarva + Jñā (knower) — 'she who knows all.'

    what she doesOmniscience as Devi. Not data-omniscience — knowing of essence. Knowing what each thing IS.

    in the bodyThird eye / direct-seeing point

  • 2
    Sarvaśakti सर्वशक्तिAll-Power

    the nameSarva + Śakti (power) — 'all-power.'

    what she doesOmnipotence as Devi. The capacity that has no limit because it does not bargain with conditions.

    in the bodyWhole body / spine

  • 3
    Sarvaiśvaryapradāyinī सर्वैश्वर्यप्रदायिनीAll-Sovereignty

    the nameSarva + Aiśvarya (sovereignty / lordship) + Pradāyinī (giver) — 'she who gives all sovereignty.'

    what she doesBestows the sovereignty of the self-recognized self. Not over others — sovereignty as self-knowing.

    in the bodyCrown / spine

  • 4
    Sarvajñānamayī सर्वज्ञानमयीAll-Knowledge

    the nameSarva + Jñāna (knowledge / awareness) + Mayī (made-of) — 'she made of all knowledge.'

    what she doesThe Devi whose substance IS knowledge. Reality recognized as awareness all the way down.

    in the bodyWhole field / awareness-substrate

  • 5
    Sarvavyādhivināśinī सर्वव्याधिविनाशिनीAll-Healing

    the nameSarva + Vyādhi (disease) + Vināśinī (destroyer) — 'she who destroys all disease.'

    what she doesHealing as recognition. The Devi who reveals that disease is the field's way of seeking recognition.

    in the bodyWherever healing is needed

  • 6
    Sarvādhārasvarūpā सर्वाधारस्वरूपाUniversal Support

    the nameSarva + Ādhāra (support / base) + Svarūpā (form) — 'she whose form is all-support.'

    what she doesThe Devi who IS the ground holding everything up. Not just supporter — the support itself.

    in the bodyMūlādhāra / soles of feet

  • 7
    Sarvapāpaharā सर्वपापहराError-Dissolution

    the nameSarva + Pāpa (sin / error / accumulated burden) + Harā (remover) — 'she who removes all sin.'

    what she doesThe Devi who dissolves the sense of having erred. Not absolution but recognition: there is no separate self to have erred.

    in the bodyShoulders / heart

  • 8
    Sarvānandamayī सर्वानन्दमयीAll-Bliss

    the nameSarva + Ānanda (bliss) + Mayī (made-of) — 'she made of all bliss.'

    what she doesThe Devi whose substance is bliss. Bliss not as feeling — as the very texture of existence.

    in the bodyWhole body / substrate-recognition

  • 9
    Sarvarakṣāsvarūpiṇī सर्वरक्षास्वरूपिणीUniversal Protection

    the nameSarva + Rakṣā (protection) + Svarūpiṇī (formed-of) — 'she whose form is all-protection.'

    what she doesThe Devi who IS the protection — not someone who protects from outside, but the held-ness itself.

    in the bodyWhole body / held-field

  • 10
    Sarvepsitaphalapradā सर्वेप्सितफलप्रदाFruit-of-Desire

    the nameSarva + Ipsita (desired) + Phala (fruit) + Pradā (giver) — 'she who gives the fruit of all that is desired.'

    what she doesThe closing Devi of the 6th avarana. Brings the fruit of every genuine longing of the practitioner.

    in the bodyHands receiving / heart receiving

From Sri Vidya, the tradition of the Sri Yantra, as it teaches them.
