Bindu Mandala ·the third enclosure

Sarvasankshobhana Chakra

All-Agitating.

8-petal lotus (Ashta Dala Padma). Holds the 8 Ananga (bodiless) Shaktis — the subtle pleasures of Manmatha/Kama beyond the physical: Ananga-Kusuma (flower), Ananga-Mekhala (girdle), Ananga-Madana (intoxication), Ananga-Madanatura (longing), Ananga-Rekha (line/streak), Ananga-Vegini (impulse), Ananga-Ankusha (goad), Ananga-Malini (garlanded). What attracts beneath what is touched.

the Shaktis of this enclosure

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Anaṅga-Kusumā अनङ्गकुसुमाSubtle Beauty

    the nameAnaṅga (bodiless) + Kusuma (flower) — 'the bodiless flower,' the flower that has no body but whose scent reaches.

    what she doesThe pleasure of beauty perceived without grasping. A flower that fills the room before you see it.

    in the bodyHeart / olfactory threshold

  • 2
    Anaṅga-Mekhalā अनङ्गमेखलाEncircling Intimacy

    the nameAnaṅga + Mekhalā (girdle / waistband) — 'the bodiless girdle,' the embrace that has no arms.

    what she doesThe pleasure of being held by something subtle. The atmosphere of a beloved presence.

    in the bodyWaist / encircling field around the body

  • 3
    Anaṅga-Madanā अनङ्गमदनाSubtle Intoxication

    the nameAnaṅga + Madana (intoxication / Kāma himself) — 'the bodiless intoxication,' intoxication without substance.

    what she doesThe light-headed clarity of being moved by what one loves. Not drunk — but altered.

    in the bodyBehind the eyes / soft palate

  • 4
    Anaṅga-Madanāturā अनङ्गमदनातुराPure Longing

    the nameAnaṅga + Madanāturā (afflicted-by-Kāma / longing) — 'the bodiless longing,' ache without object.

    what she doesThe ache that has no specific object. The longing that is itself the gift.

    in the bodyThroat / sternum

  • 5
    Anaṅga-Rekhā अनङ्गरेखाSuggested Form

    the nameAnaṅga + Rekhā (line / streak / curve) — 'the bodiless line,' the trace that suggests form.

    what she doesThe subtle line that hints at form before form arrives. A trace, a gesture, an outline.

    in the bodyEyes / spine inclining

  • 6
    Anaṅga-Veginī अनङ्गवेगिनीSubtle Velocity

    the nameAnaṅga + Vegin (swift / impulsive) + ī — 'the bodiless swift one,' velocity without substance.

    what she doesThe speed at which attraction travels. Quicker than thought, slower than reflex.

    in the bodyHeart / first reflexes

  • 7
    Anaṅga-Aṅkuśā अनङ्गाङ्कुशाSubtle Hook

    the nameAnaṅga + Aṅkuśa (goad / hook) + ā — 'the bodiless goad,' the hook without barb.

    what she doesWhat pulls you back when attention drifts. The internal call when you've wandered.

    in the bodyHeart hook / between the eyes

  • 8
    Anaṅga-Mālinī अनङ्गमालिनीSubtle Adornment

    the nameAnaṅga + Mālinī (garlanded one) — 'the bodiless garlanded,' wreathed without wreath.

    what she doesThe grace that makes everything appear adorned. The way beauty wreaths the perceived without external decoration.

    in the bodyWhole field / perceptual atmosphere
