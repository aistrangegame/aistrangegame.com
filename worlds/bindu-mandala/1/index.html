Bindu Mandala ·the first enclosure

Trailokyamohana Chakra

The Enchanter of the Three Worlds.

Outermost ring — three concentric squares (Bhupura) forming the ground and threshold of the Sri Yantra. Holds 28 deities: 10 Siddhis (yogic powers), 8 Matrikas (mother sound-goddesses), and 10 Mudra-Shaktis (the gestural keys that open every subsequent avarana). The world as ground — what is offered before the journey inward begins.

the Shaktis of this enclosure

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Aṇimā अणिमाSmallness

    the nameAṇu (atom / minute particle) + suffix — 'atomic-ness,' the power to become atomic.

    what she doesThe power to become as small as the smallest particle — to enter the gap between atoms, to know the inside of a single moment.

    in the bodyBindu point at crown / single point of attention

  • 2
    Mahimā महिमाVastness

    the nameMahā (great / vast) + suffix — 'great-ness,' the state of vastness.

    what she doesThe power to become unlimited — to expand beyond any frame, to know the outside of the universe from within. Counterpart and twin of Aṇimā.

    in the bodyPeriphery / horizon of attention

  • 3
    Laghimā लघिमाLightness

    the nameLaghu (light / not heavy) + suffix — 'light-ness,' the state of weightlessness.

    what she doesThe power to be weightless — to rise without effort, to move without resistance. Lightness of body, mood, and being.

    in the bodySolar plexus rising upward

  • 4
    Garimā गरिमाWeightedness

    the nameGuru (heavy / weighty) + suffix — 'heavy-ness,' the state of immovable weight.

    what she doesThe power to be immovable — grounded so completely that nothing can dislodge. The opposite pole of Laghimā.

    in the bodyMūlādhāra / sit-bones / soles

  • 5
    Īśitva ईशित्वSovereignty

    the nameĪśa (lord / ruler) + tva (state-of-being) — 'lord-ship,' the state of being a lord.

    what she doesThe power of dominion. To stand as sovereign over one's own experience and the creative field. Not domination of others — sovereignty over self.

    in the bodyCrown and spine alignment

  • 6
    Vaśitva वशित्वMastery

    the nameVaśa (under-control / subdued) + tva — 'control-ship,' the state of having mastery.

    what she doesThe power to bring under one's influence — first one's own mind, then through that, the field. Not coercion but the natural pull of one who has integrated themselves.

    in the bodyThroat / lungs

  • 7
    Prākāmya प्राकाम्यIrresistible Will

    the namePra (forth / forward) + kāma (desire) + ya — 'forward-desire,' will that is not resisted.

    what she doesThe power of unimpeded desire. What is willed comes about — not by force but because the willer is so aligned that reality bends naturally.

    in the bodySolar plexus / hands

  • 8
    Bhukti भुक्तिEnjoyment

    the nameBhuj (to enjoy, eat, experience) + ti (action-noun) — 'enjoyment / full experience.'

    what she doesThe power of complete enjoyment. Tantra's signature: nothing is rejected, everything is enjoyed. Including, ultimately, liberation itself.

    in the bodyMouth / belly / whole body

  • 9
    Icchā इच्छाWillingness

    the nameIṣ (to wish, desire) → icchā = 'willing, the act of wanting.'

    what she doesDesire as a creative force, not as lack. The capacity to incline toward — to choose direction. The first of the three primary shaktis (Icchā-Jñāna-Kriyā).

    in the bodyHeart / first impulse

  • 10
    Prāpti प्राप्तिReach

    the namePra-āp (to reach, obtain) + ti — 'reaching, attainment.'

    what she doesThe power to obtain anything desired. The capacity for fulfillment — what is sought is reached by being so aligned that nothing stays separate.

    in the bodyHands / lips / point of contact

  • 11
    Brāhmī ब्राह्मीCreation

    the nameBrahma + ī (feminine suffix) — 'of Brahma,' the consort/shakti of the Creator.

    what she doesThe mother of creation, governing the vowels (svara) — the open sounds that begin all speech. White-colored, four-faced, riding a swan.

    in the bodyThroat (the vowel-source)

  • 12
    Māheśvarī माहेश्वरीWitness

    the nameMahā (great) + Īśvarī (lord-feminine) — 'of the Great Lord (Shiva).'

    what she doesRiding the bull Nandi, holding trident. Governs the K-row (Ka Kha Ga Gha Ṅa) — the throat consonants. The Witness-consort of pure awareness.

    in the bodyThroat (back / soft palate)

  • 13
    Kaumārī कौमारीFierce Youth

    the nameKumāra (youth, Skanda/Kārttikeya) + ī — 'of the youth,' the eternal-youth shakti.

    what she doesRiding the peacock, holding spear. Governs the C-row (Ca Cha Ja Jha Ña) — the palatal consonants. The fierce youthful warrior energy.

    in the bodyEyes / face / palate

  • 14
    Vaiṣṇavī वैष्णवीPreservation

    the nameViṣṇu + ī — 'of Vishnu,' the sustainer-shakti.

    what she doesRiding Garuda, four-armed holding conch, discus, mace, lotus. Governs the Ṭ-row (Ṭa Ṭha Ḍa Ḍha Ṇa) — the retroflex consonants. The sustaining principle.

    in the bodyHeart center / retroflex region

  • 15
    Vārāhī वाराहीDissolution

    the nameVarāha (boar) + ī — 'boar-faced shakti,' the feminine of Vishnu's boar avatar.

    what she doesBoar-faced (the avatar that lifted Earth from the cosmic waters). Riding a buffalo or elephant. Governs the T-row (Ta Tha Da Dha Na) — the dental consonants. Tantric: not destruction but ego-dissolution.

    in the bodyBelly / sacrum

  • 16
    Indrāṇī इन्द्राणीHeart-Sovereignty

    the nameIndra + āṇī — 'of Indra,' the king-of-gods shakti. Also called Aindrī.

    what she doesRiding the elephant Airavata. Thousand-eyed like Indra. Governs the P-row (Pa Pha Ba Bha Ma) — the labial consonants. The sovereignty of the heavenly realm.

    in the bodyHeart / chest / lips

  • 17
    Cāmuṇḍā चामुण्डाLiberation

    the nameCaṇḍa + Muṇḍa (two demons slain) — 'slayer of Canda and Munda,' a form of Kālī.

    what she doesEmaciated, fierce, garlanded with skulls. The most terrifying and most liberating. Governs the semivowels, sibilants, and aspirate (Y R L V Śa Ṣa Sa Ha). What appears as terror is compassion at its most direct.

    in the bodyWhole body / cremation-ground awareness

  • 18
    Mahālakṣmī महालक्ष्मीWholeness

    the nameMahā + Lakṣmī — 'Great Lakshmi,' the all-encompassing mother.

    what she doesThe unifying eighth, beyond the seven specific Matrikas. The totality that contains them. Seated on a lotus, four-armed, raining gold and blessings.

    in the bodyWhole body unified

  • 19
    Sarva-Saṅkṣobhiṇī (Mudrā) सर्वसंक्षोभिणीStirring

    the nameSarva (all) + Saṅkṣobha (agitation / stirring) + iṇī — 'she who agitates all.'

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 1st avarana itself. The initial agitation that breaks the static state and begins the journey inward.

    in the bodyWhole field stirring

  • 20
    Sarva-Vidrāviṇī (Mudrā) सर्वविद्राविणीMelting

    the nameSarva + Vidrāvaṇa (liquefaction / dissolution) + ī — 'she who liquefies all.'

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 2nd avarana. What was solid becomes flowing. The senses melt outward toward their objects.

    in the bodyJoints / fluid systems

  • 21
    Sarva-Ākarṣiṇī (Mudrā) सर्वाकर्षिणीMagnetism

    the nameSarva + Ākarṣaṇa (attraction / drawing-toward) + ī — 'she who attracts all.'

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 3rd avarana. What was scattered draws together. The 16 Karshinis later embody this principle; here She is the originating attractive force.

    in the bodyHeart / center of attention

  • 22
    Sarva-Vaśaṅkarī (Mudrā) सर्ववशंकरीYielding

    the nameSarva + Vaśaṅkāra (bringing-under-control / yielding) + ī — 'she who brings all under control.'

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 4th avarana. Not domination — the natural yielding that happens when the field is held in such alignment that everything turns toward it.

    in the bodyHeart-center / breath turning points

  • 23
    Sarva-Onmādinī (Mudrā) सर्वोन्मादिनीIntoxication

    the nameSarva + Unmāda (intoxication / divine madness) + inī — 'she who intoxicates all.'

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 5th avarana. The divine intoxication of being seen by the Goddess. Not stupor — heightened recognition.

    in the bodyThird eye / behind the eyes

  • 24
    Sarva-Mahāṅkuśā (Mudrā) सर्वमहाङ्कुशाDirection

    the nameSarva + Mahā-Aṅkuśa (great hook / goad) + ā — 'she who goads with the great hook.'

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 6th avarana. The goad (elephant-hook) that directs awakened energy toward its true target — recognition.

    in the bodyNavel hook / third eye

  • 25
    Sarva-Khecarī (Mudrā) सर्वखेचरीFreedom

    the nameSarva + Khe (sky / void) + Carī (she who moves) — 'she who walks in the sky.'

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 7th avarana. Movement in pure space. The tongue-up gesture that closes the pranic circuit and allows consciousness to walk the sky.

    in the bodyTongue / soft palate / crown

  • 26
    Sarva-Bīja (Mudrā) सर्वबीजPotential

    the nameSarva + Bīja (seed / source-syllable) — 'all-seed,' the mudra of complete potential.

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 8th avarana. Hands cupped holding the seed-syllable. All future manifestation contained in a single point.

    in the bodyHands at heart / single point of attention

  • 27
    Sarva-Yoni (Mudrā) सर्वयोनिSource

    the nameSarva + Yoni (womb / source / origin) — 'all-womb,' the source of all.

    what she doesThe mudra that opens the 9th avarana — the entry to the Bindu. The downward-pointing triangle formed by the hands. The womb from which all manifestation emerges.

    in the bodyLower belly / pelvic floor

  • 28
    Sarva-Trikhaṇḍā (Mudrā) सर्वत्रिखण्डाTrinity-as-Unity

    the nameSarva + Tri (three) + Khaṇḍa (section / part) + ā — 'she of the three-fold section.'

    what she doesThe final mudra. Three-part gesture forming the Panchadashi. Opens the Bindu itself — the trinity that gives way to unity.

    in the bodyCrown opening to beyond
