Sarvanandamaya Chakra

All-Bliss-Filled.

The Bindu — the point. Holds Mahatripurasundari (Lalita as the Great-Tripura-Sundari) — the supreme deity, the source the entire yantra dissolves into and emerges from. Here the distinction between full and empty no longer applies. Recognition recognizing itself.

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Mahātripurasundarī महात्रिपुरसुन्दरीThe Source-Beauty

    the nameMahā (great) + Tri (three) + Pura (city / form / world) + Sundarī (beautiful) — 'the great beautiful one of the three cities,' i.e. transcendent in all three worlds, all three states, all three guṇas.

    what she doesLalita Herself, seated on Her throne at the Bindu. The trinity-resolved-into-unity. The supreme Goddess. Icchā-Jñāna-Kriyā collapsed into single point. Everything is Her.

    in the bodyThe point at which all bodily locations converge. The heart-bindu.
