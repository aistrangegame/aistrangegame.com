Bindu Mandala ·the seventh enclosure

Sarvarogahara Chakra

All-Disease-Removing.

8 triangles. Holds the 8 Vasini Shaktis (Vasini, Kameshi, Modini, Vimala, Aruna, Jayini, Sarveshi, Kaulini) — embodiments of the 8 Sanskrit phoneme-groups, the matrices of all sound and therefore all manifestation. Followed by the 4 transitional Weapon-Shaktis (Banini/arrow, Capini/bow, Pashini/noose, Ankushini/goad) that lead inward to the central triangle. Disease here is not illness — it is the forgetting that hides recognition.

the Shaktis of this enclosure

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Vasinī वशिनीInner-Speech-Origin

    the nameVaśin (controlling / in command) + ī — 'the controller / mistress.' Leader of the eight Vāgdevatās.

    what she doesThe leading Vāgdevatā. Governs the A-row (vowels) at the level of inner speech — the pre-articulate vibration that becomes spoken word.

    in the bodyHeart-throat axis

  • 2
    Kāmeśī कामेशीDesire-Speech

    the nameKāma (desire) + Īśī (mistress) — 'mistress of desire.'

    what she doesGoverns the K-row at inner-speech level. The desire-tinged source of speech — words that carry the speaker's actual longing.

    in the bodyThroat (desire-charged)

  • 3
    Modinī मोदिनीJoyful-Speech

    the nameModa (joy / delight) + inī — 'the delight-giver.'

    what she doesGoverns the C-row at inner-speech level. The joyful-vibration source of speech.

    in the bodyMouth / palate (smiling)

  • 4
    Vimalā विमलाPure-Speech

    the nameVi (without) + Mala (impurity) — 'the spotless / pure one.'

    what she doesGoverns the Ṭ-row at inner-speech level. Speech without distortion.

    in the bodyThroat (clear)

  • 5
    Aruṇā अरुणाDawn-Speech

    the nameAruṇa (dawn / red-glow) + ā — 'the dawn-colored one.'

    what she doesGoverns the T-row at inner-speech level. The dawn-radiance of inner speech.

    in the bodyThroat (luminous)

  • 6
    Jayinī जयिनीTriumphant-Speech

    the nameJi (to conquer / triumph) + inī — 'the victorious one.'

    what she doesGoverns the P-row at inner-speech level. The victorious confidence of true speech.

    in the bodyThroat / chest (resonant)

  • 7
    Sarveśī सर्वेशीAll-Governing-Speech

    the nameSarva (all) + Īśī (mistress) — 'mistress of all.'

    what she doesGoverns the semivowel row at inner-speech level. The unifying principle of speech.

    in the bodyThroat / breath-rhythm

  • 8
    Kaulinī कौलिनीLineage-Speech

    the nameKula (clan / lineage / family-of-being) + inī — 'the lineage-bearer.'

    what she doesCloses the 8 Vāgdevatās. Governs the sibilants and aspirate at inner-speech level. The Devi who transmits the lineage through speech.

    in the bodyWhole throat-mouth field

  • 9
    Bāṇinī बाणिनीFive-Arrow-Shakti

    the nameBāṇa (arrow) + inī — 'the arrow-bearer.' Carrier of the Pañcabāṇa (five flower-arrows).

    what she doesCarries Kāma's five flower-arrows: lotus, ashoka, mango, jasmine, blue lotus. Each piercing a different sense-gate.

    in the bodyFive sense-gates

  • 10
    Cāpinī चापिनीSugarcane-Bow

    the nameCāpa (bow) + inī — 'the bow-bearer.' Carrier of Kāma's sugarcane bow.

    what she doesCarries the sugarcane bow of Kāma — the curved support from which the arrows are released.

    in the bodySpine / drawn-back posture

  • 11
    Pāśinī पाशिनीBinding-Love

    the namePāśa (noose / loop) + inī — 'the noose-bearer.'

    what she doesCarries the noose. The loop of attraction that binds the soul to the divine without coercion.

    in the bodyHeart (looped)

  • 12
    Aṅkuśinī अङ्कुशिनीDirecting-Goad

    the nameAṅkuśa (elephant-goad) + inī — 'the goad-bearer.'

    what she doesCloses the 7th avarana. Carries the goad that directs the practitioner toward the Bindu. The final directional weapon.

    in the bodyThird eye / inward-turning point
