Bindu Mandala ·the eighth enclosure

Sarvasiddhiprada Chakra

All-Attainment-Bestowing.

Central triangle (Trikona) immediately surrounding the Bindu. Holds the 3 primordial Shaktis: Kameshvari (Will-to-create, source-desire, white as crystal), Vajreshi (Lightning-energy, action, red as ten million suns), Bhagamalini (Yoni-garlanded, manifestation, gold as molten amber). The trinity from which all 105 outer Shaktis emerge.

the Shaktis of this enclosure

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Kāmeśvarī कामेश्वरीWill-to-Create

    the nameKāma (desire / will) + Īśvarī (great-mistress) — 'great-mistress of desire / will.' First of the Primordial Triad.

    what she doesWhite-colored. The Icchā aspect — the will to create. Consort of Kāmeśvara (Shiva at the source). One vertex of the Mūla Trikoṇa.

    in the bodyThird eye / point-of-origin

  • 2
    Vajreśī वज्रेशीLightning-Action

    the nameVajra (lightning / diamond / thunderbolt) + Īśī (great-mistress) — 'great-mistress of the thunderbolt.' Also Vajreśvarī. Second of the Primordial Triad.

    what she doesRed-colored. The Kriyā aspect — the action-power. The thunderbolt-bearer. Second vertex of the Mūla Trikoṇa.

    in the bodyHeart / hands

  • 3
    Bhagamālinī भगमालिनीManifestation-Garland

    the nameBhaga (yoni / source-aperture / fortune) + Mālinī (garlanded) — 'she garlanded with yonis (or fortunes).' Third of the Primordial Triad.

    what she doesGolden-colored. The Jñāna aspect — the knowing-power that manifests as form. Bhaga also means good-fortune; She is garlanded with both. Third vertex of the Mūla Trikoṇa.

    in the bodyBelly / yoni / point-of-manifestation
