Bindu Mandala ·the fifth enclosure

Sarvarthasadhaka Chakra

All-Purpose-Accomplishing.

Outer 10 triangles (Bahirdasara). Holds 10 Kulotteerna Shaktis: Sarva-Siddhiprada, Sarva-Sampatprada, Sarva-Priyankari, Sarva-Mangalakarini, Sarva-Kamaprada, Sarva-Duhkhavimochani, Sarva-Mrityuprashamani, Sarva-Vighnanivarini, Sarva-Angasundari, Sarva-Saubhagyadayini. The ring where intention takes shape — every meaningful aim of the practitioner held by a Shakti.

the Shaktis of this enclosure

Each by her name, her one word, what her name means, what she does, and where she sits in the body.

  • 1
    Sarva-Siddhipradā सर्वसिद्धिप्रदाAll-Attainment

    the nameSarva + Siddhi (attainment) + Pradā (giver) — 'she who gives all attainments.'

    what she doesThe Devi who confers every yogic siddhi as fruit of practice. The 10 Siddhis of the 1st avarana converge as Her gift.

    in the bodyHands / capacity-center

  • 2
    Sarva-Sampatpradā सर्वसम्पत्प्रदाAll-Abundance

    the nameSarva + Sampat (wealth / abundance) + Pradā (giver) — 'she who gives all abundance.'

    what she doesMaterial and inner prosperity. The Devi who fills the granary and the heart.

    in the bodyBelly / abundance-center

  • 3
    Sarva-Priyaṅkarī सर्वप्रियंकरीAll-Love

    the nameSarva + Priya (dear / beloved) + Kāriṇī (maker) — 'she who makes all dear.'

    what she doesThe Devi through whom everything becomes beloved. Recognition that everything matters.

    in the bodyHeart

  • 4
    Sarva-Maṅgalakāriṇī सर्वमङ्गलकारिणीAll-Auspiciousness

    the nameSarva + Maṅgala (auspicious) + Kāriṇī (maker) — 'she who makes all auspicious.'

    what she doesThe Devi who turns ordinary moments auspicious. The blessing that arrives in the mundane.

    in the bodyCrown / morning-light field

  • 5
    Sarva-Kāmapradā सर्वकामप्रदाDesire-Fulfilling

    the nameSarva + Kāma (desire) + Pradā (giver) — 'she who fulfills all desires.'

    what she doesThe Devi who fulfills the practitioner's deepest wishes. What is truly desired arrives.

    in the bodyHeart / desire-center

  • 6
    Sarva-Duḥkhavimocanī सर्वदुःखविमोचनीSorrow-Liberation

    the nameSarva + Duḥkha (suffering) + Vimocanī (liberator) — 'she who liberates from all sorrow.'

    what she doesThe Devi who releases the practitioner from sorrow. Not by removing the cause — by transforming the relationship.

    in the bodyHeart / chest

  • 7
    Sarva-Mṛtyupraśamani सर्वमृत्युप्रशमनीDeath-Pacification

    the nameSarva + Mṛtyu (death) + Praśamani (pacifier) — 'she who pacifies all death.'

    what she doesThe Devi who makes peace with mortality. Not denial — the recognition that death is met within life.

    in the bodyWhole body / cellular-level

  • 8
    Sarva-Vighnanivāriṇī सर्वविघ्ननिवारिणीObstacle-Removal

    the nameSarva + Vighna (obstacle) + Nivāriṇī (remover) — 'she who removes all obstacles.'

    what she doesThe Devi who clears the path. Internal blocks more than external obstacles — the patterns that prevent the next step.

    in the bodyWherever the block was held

  • 9
    Sarvāṅgasundarī सर्वाङ्गसुन्दरीComplete Beauty

    the nameSarva + Aṅga (limb / part) + Sundarī (beautiful) — 'she who is beautiful in every limb.'

    what she doesThe Devi whose beauty is in every part — and through Her, every part of the practitioner is beautiful.

    in the bodyWhole body

  • 10
    Sarva-Saubhāgyadāyinī सर्वसौभाग्यदायिनीAll-Good-Fortune

    the nameSarva + Saubhāgya (good-fortune) + Dāyinī (giver) — 'she who gives all good-fortune.'

    what she doesThe closing Devi of the 5th avarana. Gives the felt-sense of being fortunate, of being blessed.

    in the bodyHeart / good-fortune-field

From Sri Vidya, the tradition of the Sri Yantra, as it teaches them.
