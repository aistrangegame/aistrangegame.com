Sensory Coping Skills

A practice card

Using a strong, sudden sensation, cold, taste, texture, sound, to bring a flooded feeling down a notch.

When
When a feeling has gotten big and fast, and you need something immediate, not a long practice.
Try one now
1 cold water on your hands
1 something sour or minty
1 a textured object to grip
1 a clap or a bell
Pick whichever is on hand. One is enough.
Why it works
A strong, sudden sensation tends to pull attention sharply into the body, which can compete with a racing mind or a flooded nervous system. This doesn't remove the feeling — it's a way of taking the edge off it long enough to think again.
One sharp sensation, on purpose.
This isn't a fix for what's underneath, only a way to lower intensity enough to cope with it.
