Barbara Marciniak

from 1988 · living · A portrait

In a workshop in Greece, a trance channeler says a group calling themselves the Pleiadians began speaking through her. She has published their words in books ever since. The source can't be checked from outside.

18 May 1988 · Athens, Greece

Contact, she said

By her account, a group calling themselves the Pleiadians began speaking through her in trance during a workshop in Greece. She had no public profile as a channeler before that day.

1988 – 1992 · United States

Hundreds of hours

She recorded and transcribed hundreds of hours of trance sessions, then shaped them into a single narrative told in the Pleiadians' own voice — addressed directly to the reader, urging humanity toward a change in awareness.

1992 · Santa Fe, New Mexico

Bringers of the Dawn

Her publisher released the book compiled from the sessions. It became one of the best-known channeled texts of its era, translated into many languages and still cited decades later.

Now

What kept going

The 1992 book has stayed in print for over three decades and sold widely, followed by further volumes she presents as also Pleiadian in origin. She continues to lecture and lead group trips to places she calls sacred, sustaining an audience well past the New Age boom that first published her.

Held open

Whether a nonphysical group actually spoke through her cannot be checked from outside; it rests on her account alone. The Pleiades are real, young, hot stars that astronomers consider far too young for complex life to have evolved there — a separate question from what she says the beings communicate.

What arrived through her in 1988 is hers to relay. It can't be proven from here.
