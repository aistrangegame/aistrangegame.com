Bashar — The Five Laws

An icebreaker

A cosmology taught through a channel since 1983 rests on five statements said to be true everywhere: you exist; everything is here and now; the One is the All and the All are the One; what you put out is what you get back; and everything changes except those four.

Ask one of these
  • If only five things were true everywhere, which would you want them to be?
  • Do you think your reality reflects your strongest beliefs — or the other way round?
  • What would you do this week if you only followed what excites you most?
Worth knowing · 1 of 5
What are the Five Laws?
You exist, always have and always will. Everything is here and now. The One is the All and the All are the One. What you put out is what you get back. Everything changes except the first four.
Worth knowing · 2 of 5
What's the difference between a law and a belief here?
A law is said to be true everywhere in creation. Everything else is a belief — and a belief can be changed.
Worth knowing · 3 of 5
What is the excitement formula?
Act on your highest excitement in every moment, take it as far as you can, and insist on nothing about the outcome. The thing that excites you may only be the delivery mechanism.
Worth knowing · 4 of 5
What did he say about 2020?
He called it the splitting prism: people choosing different paths that lead to different versions of the same world, with glass walls between them — you can see the other side, but its ideas no longer reach you.
Worth knowing · 5 of 5
Who is Bashar?
A voice channeled by Darryl Anka since 1983, described as coming from a civilisation called Essassani — which the teaching frames as a future version of ourselves.
Words to know
Permission slipAny belief, ritual, or tool that lets you allow yourself something you could already do.
SynchronicityWhat the teaching calls the organising principle of the universe — the coordination of all parts through one mind.
Splitting prismThe image for a period where realities diverge by vibration, like trains leaving a station on separate tracks.
More words
Higher mindThe part of you that already knows what to do; excitement is said to be its signal in the body.
ExpansionThe replacement for destruction at the end of a cycle: sliding sideways into the next version rather than wiping the last.
A line to leave them with
What you put out is what you get back — and the mirror won't move first.
