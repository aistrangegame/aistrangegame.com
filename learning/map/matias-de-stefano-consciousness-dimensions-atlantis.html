The Nine Dimensions

A map

As one rememberer describes them: not places but perspectives, all happening where your hands are, each a wider way of seeing from exactly where you already stand.

1

Unity

I know that I am.

Pure light, oneness experiencing itself. The centre of the circle. Visible, he says, as the dots of light that shape all things.

2

Polarity

Light and dark; yes and no.

To observe itself, unity must split. The movement of opposites — shadow, number, mathematics.

3

Experience

Height, width, depth; past, present, future.

Where everything thought and felt elsewhere becomes real. The experiment. All the other dimensions compressed into a life you can hold.

4

Time and Shape

The dreamscape.

Managing form without the constraints of time and space. Where you go in lucid dreams and in true meditation.

5

Understanding

I get it. I understand everything.

Living like energy flowing in a cable. Sacred geometry understood rather than seen.

6

Creation

The architects.

Where light becomes a rainbow and one being becomes many. The home of the twelve rays, the seven laws, the three essences.

7

Enlightenment

The lost face.

Beings of pure light who have set down their individual faces to become guides. What, from here, we call enlightenment.

8

Possibility

The dream of I AM.

Everything possible, everything stored. Where the records of all events are said to reside.

9

The Void

I know what I am.

Unity, now aware of every part of itself. Nine closes the circle that one opened.

You do not go there. You widen here.
Matías De Stefano, as he describes it — presented as memory, not evidence.
