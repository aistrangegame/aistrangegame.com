Bindu Being

A mirror, not a master

In one sentence
A life-reflection app with three faces — today, any past day, and the shape of a whole year — and deliberately no fourth: no goals, no targets, no version of you to become.
A Tuesday
Evening. I tap a few things I did and a few spaces between them — the walk, the waiting, the nothing. Tomorrow I can look at today whole, without a score. Over a month, six lights show which parts of me have been lit and which have been quiet. The goal isn't a full bar. It's all six alive.
Why I made it
Every app I'd used for this was secretly a boss: streaks, targets, the better me ahead. It never worked, because it was built on the future. I wanted one that only ever looked at what is, and trusted me to choose from there.
What's inside
Record: capture a moment with one tap — including the in-between moments most apps ignore.
Mirror: see any day back, whole, without judgement.
Scorecard: the shape across day, week, month, year — a field of six lights, never one number.
What it refuses to do
Reflect, never dictate.
No future, no 'should'.
A full day and an empty day are both just seen.
Rest is never turned into a number to maximise.
Nothing stopped is deleted. It rests.
If you want to try it
Designed in full. Not yet built. Its rules can be received without a single day of my life inside them.
