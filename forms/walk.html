Thirty Days With Anxiety

A walk

A month of short pieces, one every few days, so the teaching has to meet an ordinary life in between.

Three days are shown here. In the full walk there are thirty, and each one opens 3 days after you finish the last. You cannot binge it. That is the design.

Day 1 of 30

Why you came

Before any technique, one honest sentence: what would be different in your life if anxiety took up less room? Not "I'd be calm." Something you'd do. Write it. Everything that follows is in service of that sentence, not of feeling better for its own sake.

Carry: Carry the sentence. Say it once tomorrow morning.

Day 2 of 30

Stress, worry, anxiety — not the same thing

Stress is the body under load. Worry is the mind rehearsing. Anxiety is the alarm system firing when there is no fire. They feel similar and need different things: stress wants rest, worry wants a decision, anxiety wants the body to learn the alarm is false. Today, when the feeling comes, name which of the three it is.

Carry: Name it three times today. That's all.
I read this — start the clock
Day 3 of 30

The alarm is not the fire

The nervous system cannot tell a deadline from a predator. It runs the same programme for both — faster heart, tight chest, narrowed attention. That programme is not a malfunction; it is the oldest and most reliable thing about you. The work is not to switch it off. It is to teach it, gently and repeatedly, which alarms are false.

Carry: Next time the alarm fires, say: this is the programme, not the fire.
I read this — start the clock
The rest of the walk arrives on its own clock.

Nothing here counts your days or tells you you're behind. The gate only ever opens; it never closes on you.
