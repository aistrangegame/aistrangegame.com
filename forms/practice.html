Belly Breathing

A practice card

One breath that tells the nervous system the emergency is over. Two minutes. Anywhere.

When
When your chest is tight, your thoughts are fast, or you notice you have been holding your breath.
How
Put one hand on your chest and one on your belly.
Breathe in through the nose, slowly, and let the lower hand rise first. The upper hand stays almost still.
Breathe out through the mouth, longer than you breathed in. Let the belly fall.
Keep going. If you count, try four in, six out. If counting is stressful, don't count — just make the out-breath longer.
Now
2:00
begin

Breathe in as it grows. Out as it shrinks. The page waits with you.

After
Notice one thing that is different — the shoulders, the jaw, the speed of the thoughts. That difference is the body switching from alarm to rest. You can do this in a car, a meeting, a queue.
