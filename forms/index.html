The twelve forms

Twelve ways a piece of understanding can land. One built exemplar each — walk them, then the register assigns every topic its form.

Read
IcebreakerNeem Karoli Baba — Open a conversation: three questions, five things, five words, voices, one line.StorybookBashar — The Five Laws — A fable in ten pages: title, affirmation, text — never a summary.Teaching guideA Mirror, Not a Master — Laws, each in three registers: the law, why it exists, what it looks like on an ordinary day.
Do
Practice cardBelly Breathing — One screen to do a thing now. Its own mechanism: a timer that waits with you.WalkThirty Days With Anxiety — A gated sequence. Its own mechanism: a cooldown clock in the browser; the next day opens after the last, never before.Field guideWhen It Feels Like a Panic Attack — When → do → because. Its own mechanism: a picker at the top that jumps to the situation you are in.RecipeKombucha at Home — Plain how-to. Its own mechanism: need → do → watch for → keep, and nothing else.
