The Big Picture

A skill

The skill
The big picture is the direction underneath your goals — not what you’re trying to achieve this month, but what all of it is pointing toward. A goal finishes or fails. A direction like honesty, connection, or growth doesn’t; it’s still there either way.
What is happening
A goal fills the whole view while you’re chasing it — get it, and there’s already a new one; miss it, and it can feel like the whole picture collapsed. Stepping back to ask what direction the goal was serving keeps one result from standing in for everything.

A goal ends. A direction doesn’t.
Because a goal is a finish line, and a direction keeps going past it.
The promotion lands, and a week later there’s already a new target in view.
2 of 3
Ask what the goal is actually for.
Because most goals are stand-ins for something bigger, like connection, freedom, or being seen.
The promotion might really be chasing ‘I want to matter here,’ not the title itself.
3 of 3
The picture is bigger than any one week.
Because a single win or loss can’t hold the whole direction of a life.
One bad quarter doesn’t undo years of walking toward the same thing.
The practice
Pick a goal you’re currently chasing. Ask what it’s actually in service of — what bigger direction it points toward. Write that direction down somewhere you’ll see it after the goal is decided, either way.
The goal will resolve. The direction keeps going.
